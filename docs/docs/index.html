Live timing for endurance racing in iRacing

StintBoard runs on your PC and shows gaps, sector times, pit stops, stints, fuel and driver swaps in your browser. Free, for lap-based and timed races.

Built for long races

Everything on one screen, so you can keep your eyes on the track and still know what the race is doing.

Timing table

Positions, gaps, intervals, last and best laps, and S1, S2 and S3 sector times with purple and green highlights.

Pit stops and stints

Stop counts, pit lane times and stint lengths for every car.

Focus panel

Click any driver for gap trends, a catch estimate, and where they would rejoin if they pitted now.

Driver swaps

See how long each team driver has spent in the car, and how long the current stint has run.

Fuel planner

For your own car: burn per lap, fuel to add to finish, and how many stops are still needed.

Track map

Drawn from your own first lap, with every car shown on it.

Class filter

Multiclass race? Filter the table to one class and see class positions.

Lap and timed races

Works for both. It shows laps remaining or the clock, whichever the session uses.

Up and running in a minute

There's nothing to install and no account to make.

  1. Download and unzip

    Get the zip file from the download section below and unzip it anywhere on your PC.

  2. Start iRacing

    Load into a practice, qualifying or race session.

  3. Open StintBoard

    Double-click StintBoard.exe. Your browser opens the dashboard by itself. For a phone or tablet on the same Wi-Fi, open your PC's address on port 8080.

Good to know

  • Windows will probably say "Windows protected your PC" the first time. Click More info, then Run anyway. The app isn't code-signed.
  • iRacing doesn't share fuel for other cars, so the fuel planner covers your car only.
  • Gap trends need about 3 laps of data before they show.
